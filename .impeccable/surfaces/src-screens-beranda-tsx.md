# Beranda (home)

Scope: the app's home screen and the visual world for the whole app. Mode: Operate. Phone first (390 × 844), centered column on larger screens.

Audience & job: a tired parent, one hand free, often at night. In under 2 minutes: tick today's stimulation, log counters with one tap, see the next immunization, reach danger signs instantly.

Proof/content: real Buku KIA activities with page numbers, real Kemenkes/IDAI dose windows from the vaccine engine, real counter norms. Never invented claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Today's stimulation is one balanced plate: six development-domain sectors that fill with flat colour as Ayah/Bunda tick activities. Refuses the pastel card-stack-with-greeting the category ships.

OWN-WORLD: Kemenkes "Isi Piringku" public-health infographic. Flat, unmodulated colour fields, no gradients, shadows or grain. Chrome in one cool neutral scale (ink #1F2A30 on #F4F6F3, white panels). Six domain hues are the only colour besides state: sosial #E2557F, bahasa #F09A2A, kognitif #7357D1, motorik kasar #2E9A66, motorik halus #2685CF, kemandirian #B8921C; danger red only for "Tanda bahaya". One sans (Figtree), tabular figures everywhere numbers move.

STORY: Parent opens the app, sees how full today's plate is, ticks what they did, taps counters, notices the vaccine window, and leaves. Nothing scores or shames; a day counts when one core + one rotating activity is ticked.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header: child name + age left, red "Tanda bahaya" pill right. Plate (160 px) left with "2 dari 5" and a 7-day strip of identical mini plates right. Below: white list of today's activities, each with a domain bar, title, meta, 34 px tick circle (48 px hit). Then the next-immunization row with status chip and "Paling lambat". Bottom third: five ink counter tiles (label, tabular count, "terakhir"). Tab bar.

FORM: Isi Piringku plate world, my grounded list position 4 of 7, seed: seed:1493f1222af78b8d. Signature move: ticking an activity fills its domain sector on the plate (and today's mini plate) with a short settle; reduced motion fills instantly.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
